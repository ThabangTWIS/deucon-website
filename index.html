About Deucon

A focused consultancy built on hands-on engineering.

Deucon was founded by Gavin Meyer to give clients direct access to experienced electrical engineering, without the layers of a large firm. We work alongside architects, developers, facility owners and contractors to deliver electrical installations that are safe, efficient and built to last.

From reticulation and building services to solar PV and backup power, we take responsibility for the design and stay involved through construction, testing and handover.

Safety and compliance first

Every design is checked against current regulations, standards and the local authority's requirements.

One point of contact

You deal with the engineer doing the work, from the first meeting to the final certificate.

Value over the life of the asset

We weigh capital cost against energy use, maintenance and future expansion.

Services

Electrical engineering across the full project lifecycle.

Engage us for a single stage or the whole project. Scopes are tailored to the size and risk of each job.

Building services design

Complete electrical design for commercial, residential and institutional buildings.

  • Lighting and small power layouts
  • Distribution boards and cable sizing
  • Data, access control and CCTV reticulation
  • Lightning and surge protection

Power distribution and MV/LV

Supply applications, substations and reticulation for sites and developments.

  • Bulk supply applications to the utility
  • Miniature substations and transformers
  • MV and LV reticulation networks
  • Load studies, fault levels and protection

Renewable and backup power

Solar PV, battery storage and generator systems that keep the lights on.

  • Grid-tied and hybrid solar PV design
  • Battery energy storage sizing
  • Standby generator integration
  • SSEG registration support

Compliance and inspections

Independent checks that installations are safe and meet the standards.

  • Condition assessments and audits
  • SANS 10142 compliance reviews
  • Occupancy and handover inspections
  • Remedial scopes and reports

Energy efficiency

Practical measures that reduce consumption and peak demand.

  • Energy audits and metering strategies
  • LED lighting retrofits
  • Power factor correction
  • Load management and tariff reviews

Project and contract management

Tendering, site supervision and close-out on your behalf.

  • Tender documents and bills of quantities
  • Contractor evaluation and appointment
  • Site meetings and quality control
  • Commissioning and as-built records

Leadership

Gavin Meyer

Gavin brings years of consulting electrical engineering experience to every Deucon project. Before founding Deucon in 2026, he spent seven years as a director of a Durban consulting engineering practice, after senior engineering roles at established South African and multinational consultancies.

He has delivered the full project cycle many times over: preliminary and detailed design, specifications and bills of quantities, public tender documents and evaluations, site supervision, contractor payment certification, and close-out with as-built drawings and O&M manuals.

Career highlights

  • Witness testing of MV switchgear, LV distribution boards, standby generators and pump station control boards
  • Substation earth mat resistance and lightning protection testing
  • Load flow and fault level studies, including a 20-year rural electrification master plan
  • Maximum demand control study for the potrooms of an aluminium smelter
  • Public sector tenders, bid documentation and tender evaluation reports
  • Leading multidisciplinary teams with architects and civil engineers, and managing clerks of works on site
